# ShopScope — Customer validation playbook
Status: not conducted. Do not fabricate responses or payments.

## Recruit and interview
Recruit 15 active small Vietnamese sellers, preferably in mother-and-baby. Obtain consent, anonymize IDs and keep notes private. Ask about the most recent pricing decision, actual research steps and tools, elapsed time, comparison errors, fee and margin calculations, and current spending on seller tools. Only then show the prototype. Ask what was confusing or missing rather than leading them to praise it.

## Paired task
Recruit 10 eligible sellers. Prepare matched tasks and human-verified reference answers, including an insufficient-evidence scenario. Counterbalance manual and prototype order. Record completion seconds, correct product identity, viable price floor, confidence and correct abstention. A faster but wrong answer fails. Report within-person changes, denominators and failures.

## Paid offer
Only after data permission and a credible refresh plan, offer a defined one-month paid pilot at VND 99,000. Separate actual payment/signed paid commitment from verbal willingness. Log real outcomes, exclusions and objections.

## Review gates
- Problem: 10 of 15 describe recurring pricing difficulty; otherwise revisit customer/problem.
- Product: median task time falls by at least 50% without lower accuracy; otherwise improve workflow or data.
- Revenue: 3 of 15 eligible sellers commit to a real paid pilot; otherwise revise price or value proposition.
- Data: reviewed SKU equivalence and licensed refreshed prices are commercial release gates.

Use the empty CSV template locally. Assign actual named experiment owners and review dates before recruiting. Do not publish personally identifying data.
